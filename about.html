A Note on This Guide

Most bachelorette groups on Isle of Palms book a big house, spend the day on the sand, and then try to figure out the night. The island has a handful of good bars and not much else after dark, so we put the useful stuff in one place: what's on Front Beach, what's across the bridge, and who comes to the house.

Local performers we work with include Michael At Your Place, a Charleston-based entertainer who's a regular at Isle of Palms bachelorette parties.

If you'd like us to set up a show at your rental, the details are on the private party page. All guests must be 18 or older.
